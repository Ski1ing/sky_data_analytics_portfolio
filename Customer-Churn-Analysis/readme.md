# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn data using Microsoft Excel to identify factors that influence customer retention.
It includes data cleaning, PivotTables, interactive dashboard, and business insights to help understand customer behavior and churn patterns.

Dataset: [Telco Customer Churn Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Excel Formulas

## Data Cleaning
- Check for missing values
- Reviewed for duplicate values
- Standardized data formatting
- Verified data consistency
- Created helper columns for analysis
- Grouping customers based on tenure
- Converting Senior Citizen values from '0' and '1' to 'No' and 'Yes'

## Business Insights
- Out of 7,043 customers, 1,896 customers churned, resulting in an overall churn rate of 26.54%. This indicates that approximately one out of four customers left the company, highlighting customer retention as a significant area of concern.
- Customers with a month-to-month contracts account for the largest number of churned customers, while customers with two-year contracts account for very few. This suggests that long-term contracts may be associated with stronger customer retention and could be explored as part of a retention strategy.
- 0-12 months tenure group has a high number of churned customers compared to customers in longer tenure groups. This indicates that the early stages of of the customers lifecycle may be particularly important, and improving onboarding, service experience, early retention efforts could help prevent customer loss.
- Fiber Optic customers has a substantial number of churned customers despite being a popular internet service category. This could be explored further to determine factors such as pricing, service quality, or whether customer expectations are being met.
